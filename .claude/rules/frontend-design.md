# Frontend Design & UI/UX Rules (Anti-Generic UI)

## Subject-Grounded Design
- Adapt aesthetic choices directly to the subject matter (Elden Ring / Dark Fantasy: gold/runic accents, dark stone backgrounds, Cinzel & Inter typography).
- Never use generic SaaS templates or generic dashboard looks for game tools.

## Avoid AI Design Tells
- **No SaaS Card Kit:** Do not box every single piece of content into identical rounded cards with identical gray shadows and gradient washes.
- **No Decorative Animation Clutter:** Avoid fade-and-slide-up on every element or hover bounces everywhere. Only use motion in direct response to user interaction (opening modals, toggling state, active marker transitions).
- **Typography Restraint:**
  - Avoid tracked-out ALL-CAPS eyebrow labels above every header.
  - Avoid highlighting single words with arbitrary italics or accent colors.
  - Keep text line lengths under 80 characters for comfortable reading.
- **Information Hierarchy:**
  - Structure (borders, dividers, badges) must convey actual status or categories, not decorative chrome.
  - Spend visual boldness in one focal area; keep secondary elements clean and disciplined.
